Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you log: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, store your expenses, and show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and protected with reasonable security measures, including hashed passwords. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts. Beyond that, we do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at the support address provided on our website.
